Cozy premium 3D clay toy illustration, lavender knitted neck scarf. Gentle diffuse lighting, milk cream peach pastel. IMPORTANT production constraint: kitten face has NO EYES drawn, no eyebrows, no sockets, no eyelids; leave clean plain cream fur in the two eye positions because interactive eyes will be composited in code. Nose and mouth ARE present. Straight front-facing symmetry, full unobstructed head.
